Digital Business Card

A proposal to let every member share their contact details from their phone in a few seconds, with no printed cards and nothing for the other person to install.

Proposal v1.0  ·  October 2026  ·  Feature: 006-digital-business-card

How It Works

Popular tools like HiHello, Popl and Blinq all work the same way, and members will find it familiar. Each member has a card page. They share it by showing a QR code, and the other person scans it with their phone camera. No app and no account are needed on the receiving end.

1. Show

The member taps "Show my card" and a full-screen QR code appears.

2. Scan

The other person points their phone camera at the screen.

3. View

The member's card page opens: name, business, role, and the contact details they chose to share.

4. Save

One tap on "Save contact" adds them to the phone's contacts.

The link can also be sent by text or email. The card always shows current information, so members never need to reprint or resend anything.

Getting to the Card Quickly

A card is only useful if it comes up the moment someone asks for it. Typing an email and password in the middle of a conversation would defeat the purpose, so we propose two changes that work together.

A. "Show my card" works even when signed out

After a member signs in once, the app remembers their card on that phone. A "Show my card" button on the sign-in screen displays the QR code immediately, with no password and no internet connection needed. This is safe because the card only contains what the member chose to make public.

B. Stay signed in, with Face ID or fingerprint

Members stay signed in for longer, and the app opens with Face ID or a fingerprint, the way banking apps do. Today, sessions last up to 30 days if "Remember me" is checked and 24 hours if not. This makes the whole app smoother, not only the card.

Decisions We Need From You

Question Options and our recommendation
☐ 1. Whose card is it? The directory today shows business pages. We recommend a card for each individual person (name, role, their business), so that team members at the same business each have their own.
☐ 2. What appears on the card? We recommend each member chooses which details to share (for example phone, email, website), with a switch to turn the card off entirely. Alternative: the chamber sets one fixed layout for everyone.
☐ 3. Quick access We recommend both A and B above. They cover the case where a member isn't signed in and the everyday case of getting into the app quickly.
☐ 4. Home screen widget A widget would put the QR code one tap from the phone's home screen. It must be built separately for iPhone and Android, so it is a meaningful extra project. We suggest deferring it until members have used the card.
