Richfield Area Chamber of Commerce — Membership App

Business-Centric Member Directory

Feature behavior review for client sign-off. This document describes how the updated member directory and profile system will work from the perspective of chamber staff, member businesses, and app users.

Version 1.0  ·  July 2026  ·  Feature: 003-company-based-membership

What Is Changing and Why

Today, the member directory is built on top of individual contacts in GoHighLevel. This means that when you look up a member in the app, you are really seeing a person's contact record — with a business name field typed in by hand.

The updated system flips this: the business is the member. Each card in the member directory represents a GHL Business record — the company that holds the chamber membership. The people who work there (and who log into the app) are connected to that business inside GoHighLevel.

What stays the same from the chamber's perspective

The public-facing labels do not change. Visitors still see the "Member Directory." Listings still show business names, logos, and contact details. The app's navigation and overall look remain the same.

Old model vs. new model at a glance

Aspect Current (contact-based) Updated (business-based)
What represents a "member" A GHL Contact record A GHL Business record
What an app user is The same GHL Contact A GHL Contact linked to a Business
Multiple staff per member Not supported cleanly Multiple contacts can be linked to one Business in GHL
Who can edit the member profile The one contact whose ID matches Any user associated with that member business who is either the main contact or a business profile editor, or any chamber/app admin
Member directory card Contact's name + typed "business name" field The GHL Business record's name and contact details

All existing profile data (photos, bios, social links, etc.) already stored in GoHighLevel will be automatically migrated to the new Business Profile records. Members will not need to fill anything in again.

Roles and Permissions

There are five levels of access. The chamber admin controls the top two levels from within GoHighLevel. The "main contact" for each business controls the fourth level from within the app.

Role Edit business profile Add team members Grant / revoke editor role How it's assigned
Chamber admin ✓ Yes ✓ Yes ✓ Yes GHL account access
App admin ✓ Yes ✓ Yes ✓ Yes App admin role
Main contact ✓ Yes ✓ Yes ✓ Yes Chamber admin applies main-contact tag in GHL
Business profile editor ✓ Yes ✓ Yes — No Main contact grants access via the app (or chamber admin via GHL tag)
Other team member — No — No — No Linked to the business in GHL but no edit tag applied

How the main contact works

Each business has exactly one main contact — the person primarily responsible for the membership. The chamber admin designates this person by applying a main-contact tag to their GHL Contact record. This is done entirely within GoHighLevel; no action is required in the app.

The main contact:

  • Always has permission to edit the Member Business Profile.
  • Can view a "Manage Team" panel on their business profile page showing all staff linked to their business in GHL.
  • Can grant or revoke the "Business Profile Editor" role for any of their team members, directly from within the app.
  • Cannot reassign or remove their own main-contact status — only the chamber admin can do that in GHL.

How the business profile editor role works

The main contact can designate any colleague linked to their business as a business profile editor. That person can then edit the Member Business Profile just like the main contact can, and can also add new team members to the business. There is no limit to how many people can hold this role for one business.

Only the main contact and app admins can grant or revoke the Business Profile Editor role for team members. Revoking access takes effect immediately.

Permission changes take effect at next login

When the chamber admin changes a contact's main-contact status in GHL, that change takes effect the next time the affected person logs into the app. Editor grants made by the main contact within the app take effect immediately.

Member Directory

The member directory page continues to show the same grid of member cards. The visible layout does not change for app visitors.

What changes behind the scenes

  • Each card now represents a GHL Business record instead of a contact record.
  • The business name, contact details, and address on each card come directly from the GHL Business object.
  • Extended data (logo, cover image, tagline, social links, etc.) comes from the new Business Profile record in the app's database.

How a business appears in the directory

A business is listed in the member directory when the chamber admin has created a Business record in GoHighLevel and a Business Profile record exists in the app with a membership tier set. The chamber admin sets or changes the membership tier through the admin panel in the app (same as today).

Filtering and search

Filtering by business category, membership tier, and keyword search continues to work exactly as it does today.

Member Business Profile Page

Clicking on a member card opens the business profile page. This page shows all information about that business.

Viewing the profile (all logged-in users)

  • Business name, description, tagline, address, phone, email, website
  • Business logo and cover banner image
  • Social media links
  • Business categories
  • Membership information (tier and member-since date — can be hidden by the business)
  • Job openings posted by that business

Editing the profile (main contact and business profile editors only)

When a user with edit permission views their own business's profile, an "Edit Profile" button appears. Clicking it enables an inline edit form covering all of the fields listed above. Changes are saved back to GoHighLevel and to the app's database simultaneously.

Manage Team panel (users with edit permission)

Any user with business profile edit permission — the main contact, business profile editors, and app admins — sees a "Manage Team" section on the business profile page. This section lists every GHL Contact linked to the business and allows adding new team members. Controls to grant or revoke the Business Profile Editor role are visible only to the main contact and app admins.

Adding a new team member

Clicking "Add Team Member" opens a form. On submit, the app performs these steps in order:

  1. Create a new GHL Contact record with the provided contact fields
  2. Link the contact to the business by setting their businessId in GHL
  3. Apply the active tag to the contact — required for app login
  4. Set the contact's renewal_date custom field to today's date — required for app login
  5. Apply the same membership package tag the business currently holds (e.g. enhanced membership package) — so the new team member inherits the business's tier
  6. If the "Grant Business Profile Editor access" toggle is on (main contacts and app admins only): apply the business-profile-editor tag
  7. Send an invite email to the new team member (see below)

The new person appears in the Manage Team list immediately.

The form includes the following fields:

Field Required? Notes
First name Required Stored on the GHL Contact record
Last name Required Stored on the GHL Contact record
Email address Required Becomes the team member's app login email; must be unique in GHL
Phone number Optional Stored on the GHL Contact record
Job title Optional e.g. "Sales Manager" — stored on the GHL Contact record
Grant Business Profile Editor access Optional Toggle — if on, the business-profile-editor tag is applied to the new contact in GHL immediately. Visible only to the main contact and app admins.

Managing editor access for existing team members

For each person already linked to the business, the full team list is visible to any user with edit permission. The toggle to grant or revoke the business-profile-editor tag is available only to the main contact and app admins. Changes take effect immediately.

The main contact always appears in the list but their editor access cannot be toggled — it derives from the main-contact tag, which only the chamber admin controls in GoHighLevel.

Invite email sent to new team members

After a new team member is created, the app automatically sends them an invite email. The email contains a "Connect Your Account" button that links to the Connect Account page with their email address pre-filled in the URL:

https://richfieldareachamber.com/connect-account?email=their.email%40example.com

When the new team member clicks the link, the Connect Account page opens with their email address already filled in and locked. They then set a password to complete their app account setup.

My Profile Page

The personal "My Profile" page (accessible from the avatar menu) becomes a leaner, person-focused page. All business-related fields move to the Member Business Profile.

What remains on My Profile

  • First and last name
  • Personal email address (displayed but not editable here — changing it would affect login)
  • Personal phone number
  • Job title (e.g. "Owner", "Sales Manager")
  • Short personal bio
  • Personal LinkedIn URL
  • Personal headshot / avatar
  • Password change

 

My Business card

A prominent "My Business" card is added to the My Profile page. It shows the name of the business the logged-in user belongs to and provides a direct link to the Member Business Profile page. If the user has edit permission, the card also shows an "Edit Member Business Profile" shortcut button.

Users without a linked business

If a contact has not yet been linked to a Business in GoHighLevel, the "My Business" card will show a placeholder explaining that the chamber admin needs to link them to their business record.

Admin — User & Membership Management

The admin panel's Users section is redesigned into three separate tabs, each sourcing data from a different place. App admins also gain an "Add New Membership" button to create a complete member record — contact, business, and association — directly from the app without switching to GoHighLevel.

Three-tab overview

Tab Data source What it shows
Business Memberships GHL Businesses API Every GHL Business record for this location — one row per business, with tier and member-since info from the local BusinessProfile table
Contacts GHL Contacts API (filtered) GHL Contact records that have a businessId set — showing which people are associated with a business and what roles they hold
App Users App database People who have registered an account in the app — with indicators for whether they are linked to a GHL Contact and GHL Business

Business Memberships tab

Lists every GHL Business in the chamber's account. This is the primary view for tracking who is a member and at what tier.

  • Search: by business name
  • Filter by tier: Basic, Enhanced, Elite, or No tier set (not yet shown in directory)
  • Filter by city / state: narrows to a specific geographic area

Each row shows: business name, membership tier, city, member-since date, number of GHL Contacts linked to the business, and an action menu. Actions available per row:

  • Set or change membership tier (writes to local BusinessProfile)
  • View the business's public profile in the app
  • Open the business record in GoHighLevel (external link)

Contacts tab

Lists GHL Contact records that are associated with a business. Contacts without a businessId are excluded from this view — they would appear as unlinked contacts in GoHighLevel and do not have a member relationship.

  • Search: by contact name or email address
  • Filter by business: show only contacts belonging to a specific business
  • Filter by role tag: Main Contact, Business Profile Editor, or No special role

Each row shows: contact name, email, phone, linked business name, and any role tags (main-contact, business-profile-editor). A visual indicator shows whether this contact has a corresponding app user account.

Actions available per row:

  • Apply or remove the main-contact tag (calls GHL tag update API)
  • Apply or remove the business-profile-editor tag
  • Open the contact record in GoHighLevel (external link)

App Users tab

Lists people who have created an account in the app — the same table that exists in the admin panel today. Search and filter options carry over from the current implementation:

  • Search: by name, email, or business name
  • Filter by role: Admin, Moderator, Board Member, Member
  • Filter by status: Active, Pending, Suspended

Each row shows: name, email, app role, account status, membership tier (from linked business), and date joined. A visual indicator shows whether the account is linked to a GHL Contact and whether that contact is linked to a GHL Business.

Add New Membership

An "Add New Membership" button appears at the top of the Users admin section. It is visible to app admins only. Clicking it opens a form divided into two sections:

On submit, the app performs these steps in order and rolls back on failure:

  1. Create the GHL Contact (POST /contacts) with the contact section fields
  2. Create the GHL Business (POST /businesses/) with the business section fields
  3. Associate the contact with the business by updating the contact's businessId in GHL
  4. Apply the active tag to the contact
  5. Set the contact's renewal_date custom field to today's date (or the Member Since date if one was provided) — this is required for the contact to be able to log into the app
  6. If a membership tier was selected, apply the matching membership package tag (basic membership package, enhanced membership package, or elite membership package) — this is what makes the business appear in the member directory
  7. If "Mark as main contact" is on, apply the main-contact tag to the GHL Contact
  8. If a membership tier was selected, upsert the local BusinessProfile row with that tier and member-since date
  9. Send an invite email to the new contact with a "Connect Your Account" link that pre-fills their email address on the Connect Account page:
    https://richfieldareachamber.com/connect-account?email=their.email%40example.com

 

Tags required for app login

A GHL contact must have the active tag and a renewal_date custom field that is no more than 13 months old before they can log into the app. The "Add New Membership" form sets both automatically. If either is missing, the contact will receive an "Account not active" error when they try to log in.

After the form is saved, the new business appears in the Business Memberships tab and the new contact appears in the Contacts tab immediately.

Existing workflow in GoHighLevel still works

The chamber admin can continue to create businesses and contacts directly in GoHighLevel as before. The app reads from GHL on every page load, so any records created in GHL will automatically appear in the admin tabs without any additional action in the app.

The chamber admin can hide the membership tier label from a business's public profile. That preference is set by the business itself via the Privacy toggle on the Member Business Profile edit form.

Data Migration

All existing member profile data will be automatically migrated when this feature goes live. No action is required from member businesses.

What gets migrated automatically

  • Business logos (avatar images) and cover photos
  • Taglines and extended business descriptions (bios)
  • Social media links (Facebook, Instagram, X / Twitter, LinkedIn)
  • Member-since dates and membership tier assignments
  • Coupon codes
  • Specialties and organization type
  • Business category selections
  • GHL Business records created from existing contact data and contacts linked to their business automatically

What the development team does before go-live (automated)

GHL Business records are created automatically by a one-time migration script. The script reads every active contact in GoHighLevel, groups them by business name, and creates a GHL Business record for each unique business — then links each contact to their business. No manual data entry is needed for this step.

The script also reviews the existing contact data and flags any contacts that have no business name, so those edge cases can be handled separately.

What the chamber admin needs to do before go-live

  1. Review the migration script's output and confirm the business records and contact groupings look correct. If any business name was misspelled or inconsistently entered across contacts, correct it in GoHighLevel before the script runs.

That's it. main-contact tags are already applied to the appropriate contacts. Additional business profile editors can be granted by each main contact from within the app at any time after go-live — no pre-launch action needed.

What happens to contacts not linked to a business?

Any active contact that had no business name on their GHL record will be flagged in the migration script output. These contacts can still log in and use the app — they will see a "My Business" card on their profile that says their account hasn't been linked to a business yet. The chamber admin can link them manually in GoHighLevel at any time.
